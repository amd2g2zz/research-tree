# Adaptive Research Policy

## Why

Recursive growth makes opaque local choices from incomplete evidence. The runtime needs deterministic, lineage-bound policy values and a non-authoritative compatibility boundary before choices can guide the coordinator.

## What Changes

- Add typed evidence-bound proposals with deterministic replay and pruning.
- Replace scalar delta with an attributable six-component vector.
- Validate versioned, lineage-rich Insight Digests.
- Demote recursive completion/delivery to observations and blockers.
- Record focused pytest, Ruff lint, and format checks for every TDD slice.

## Scope

Runtime modules are policy, delta, insights, and recursive projection, plus focused tests and group 6/16 receipts. No persistence, dispatch, host, alignment, invalidation, capture, or portfolio authority is added.
