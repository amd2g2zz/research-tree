## Why

Prose-only strategy tracks could collapse into one Decision Slot, and native
dependency syntax allowed arbitrary serial chains.

## What Changes

- Add typed strategy tracks with priority, closure oracle, and evidence
  boundary to alignment compilation.
- Require every active track to have an executable research question and every
  slot to identify its track.
- Require dependency kind, rationale, and evidence reference at native task
  registration.
- Expose ready-wave tracks and blocked dependency justifications in status and
  receipt-backed delivery reports.
