## Why

Delivery currently permits host-level Markdown heuristics to stand in for
semantic quality and treats the human artifact as a secondary brief. Alpha2
needs two co-primary, evidence-derived artifacts whose exact revisions and
acceptance remain governed by the canonical coordinator.

## What Changes

- Rename Human Brief to Human Research Report with explicit read compatibility.
- Compile both delivery artifacts from the same canonical research revisions.
- Replace size/heading gates with traceability and implementation-usefulness
  requirements for consequential claims.
- Bind acceptance or rejection to exact artifact and decision revisions.
- Persist rejection-driven successor work and prevent adapter-owned completion.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `semantic-dual-delivery`: Co-primary, semantically validated Technical
  Research Package and Human Research Report production and acceptance.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

This changes delivery and acceptance contracts, coordinator delivery lifecycle,
host projections, JSON schemas/examples, tests, and the Alpha2 group-10
verification receipt. Existing immutable delivery revisions remain readable.
