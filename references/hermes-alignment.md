# Hermes Alignment Phase

Load this file only while the research strategy is still being formed or has
been reopened by new evidence or requester feedback.

## Mutual cognition loop

Run short, recursive turns. In each turn:

1. Inspect the Context Pack before eliciting details that can be learned.
2. Record the agent's current reading as a hypothesis: outcome, target user,
   expected artifact, scope, authority, environment, and success evidence.
3. Run bounded reconnaissance against the single gap most likely to redirect
   the strategy. Prefer repository evidence, primary sources, and a small safe
   experiment over generic advice.
4. Tell the requester what was learned, why it matters, and one place the
   agent's reading may be wrong.
5. Invite correction, context, or counterargument in the requester's own words.
6. Test the reply rather than accepting every technical assertion as fact.
7. Persist both human and agent belief deltas and the decision effect.

Do not force the requester to operate a research schema. Keep internal ledgers
internal unless a small excerpt helps thinking.

## Blind-spot reconnaissance

Cover only gaps capable of changing the plan:

- desired operational outcome versus a named technology;
- actual user and workflow;
- inputs, outputs, integration boundary, and lifecycle;
- quality and evidence threshold;
- available authority, execution environment, network, and data;
- feasibility constraints and unsafe or irreversible operations;
- baseline, alternatives, and evaluation oracle.

Return a short Blind-Spot Packet only as an interim teaching device. It must
contain current reading, evidence gained, consequence, provisional intent
rewrite, proposed scope expansion, disagreements, and the next open-ended
reflection. It is never the final report.

## Feasibility

Classify constraints as hard, preference, aspiration, or estimate. Test their
combination against evidence. Do not call a request impossible from intuition,
and do not produce an elaborate plan for a physically or technically
inconsistent target. When evidence is insufficient, run the smallest spike
that can change the disposition.

Use one of: `plausible`, `conditionally-plausible`, `indeterminate`, or
`infeasible`. For an infeasible outcome, show the conflicting constraints and
nearest feasible reframings, but do not silently choose a substitute.

## Strategy equilibrium

Handoff is allowed only when:

- the user and agent can each see how their model changed;
- the outcome, scope, non-goals, artifact, authority, environment, and evidence
  standard are explicit;
- feasibility is supported rather than assumed;
- the completion oracle is operational and observable;
- no open disagreement can materially redirect research; and
- the requester understands the Human Brief shape that will accompany the
  technical package.

Execute-direct permits a provisional checkpoint and immediate handoff, but it
does not waive authority, safety, feasibility, or honest completion rules.
